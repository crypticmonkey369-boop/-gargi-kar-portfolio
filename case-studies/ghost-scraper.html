The Ghost Scraper

Breaking the "Fragility Barrier" in Competitor Intelligence. Vision-based price tracking that renders code obfuscation completely irrelevant.

The Business Friction

For most e-commerce brands, competitor price tracking is a "cat-and-mouse" game. Traditional scrapers rely on CSS Selectors—the underlying code of a website. The moment a competitor changes a single line of code or shifts a button two pixels to the left, the scraper breaks.

Our client was losing 12+ hours a week manually fixing broken scripts and verifying data across 12 different dynamic storefronts. They weren't just losing time; they were losing the ability to price-match in real-time, resulting in lost sales.

The "Aha!" Insight

"If a human eye can see the price, an AI eye can too."

Instead of building a script that looks for code, I built an agent that looks for patterns. By moving away from "Code-Dependency" and moving toward "Visual-Context," we rendered the competitor's anti-scraping updates completely irrelevant.

The Architecture

1

Visual Capture

Triggers a headless browser to take high-resolution "segment snapshots" of competitor product pages

2

Vision-Reasoning

Gemini 1.5 Flash "scans" the image to identify price, currency, and stock status based on visual hierarchy

3

Data Validation

Extracted data is cross-referenced against the internal SKU library and pushed directly into the pricing engine
