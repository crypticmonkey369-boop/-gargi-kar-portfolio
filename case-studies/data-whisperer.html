The Data Whisperer

Breaking the "SQL Gatekeeper" with Natural Language BI. Ask questions in plain English—get boardroom-ready charts in 10 seconds.

The Information Embargo

A D2C founder was "data-rich but insight-poor." Strategic questions required a 48-hour lag time while developers pulled SQL reports. The founder felt blind in meetings, and the developer felt like a "data puller."

Every business question became a bottleneck. Not because the data didn't exist, but because accessing it required a technical middleman. Decision-making was slowed by the very systems meant to accelerate it.

Talk to Your Data

I engineered a Natural Language Semantic Layer using n8n and NocoDB, powered by Gemini 1.5 Pro. The founder can now "talk to their data" in plain English—and get answers in seconds, not days.
